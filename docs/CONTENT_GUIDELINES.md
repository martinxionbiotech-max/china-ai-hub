# CONTENT_GUIDELINES.md — China AI Hub

Editorial standards for guides, technology pages, comparisons, and research articles.

## 1. Writing voice

Senior AI industry researcher + technical analyst + independent English-language author.

- **Clear, direct, analytical, professional.** Technical when necessary, human always.
- **Substance over length.** A 1,500-word article with original analysis beats 4,000 words of repetition. Never inflate word count.
- **Evidence-driven.** Analytical claims are traceable to cited sources.

### Banned phrasing
"AI is rapidly transforming…", "In today's fast-paced world…", "With the development of AI…", "It is important to note that…", "revolutionary", "game-changing", "unprecedented" — unless genuinely supported and necessary. Also banned: repetitive AI-generated phrasing, hollow transitions, filler adjectives.

## 2. Claim classification

Inline labels used throughout editorial content:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| FACT | Verified against L1/L2 source |
| VENDOR CLAIM | Stated by vendor, not independently verified |
| INDEPENDENT FINDING | From independent evaluation/research |
| ANALYSIS | Our reasoning over facts |
| INTERPRETATION | Our reading of what facts mean |
| ESTIMATE | Derived value, clearly approximate |
| UNCERTAINTY | Conflicts or missing data |

Interpretation is never presented as fact.

## 3. Article framework (reasoning guide, not a template)

H1 → Executive Summary → What We Know → What the Data Shows → What Has Changed → Why It Matters → Detailed Analysis → Comparison/Evidence → Limitations and Uncertainty → China AI Hub View → Conclusion → Sources.

Do not mechanically force every heading into every article. The logic chain must be: Information → Evidence → Analysis → Interpretation → Conclusion → Original Synthesis → Author View.

## 4. Originality test (mandatory before publishing any substantial article)

1. What information was independently researched?
2. What data was collected?
3. What comparison was performed?
4. What analysis was performed?
5. What is genuinely new?
6. What is the author's independent interpretation?
7. Could this article exist without simply rewriting another source?

If the answer to #7 is "no": DO NOT PUBLISH. Revise.

### What original content is NOT
- Translated Chinese articles
- Rewritten company documentation
- AI-generated summaries
- Content assembled from five articles
- Keyword-expanded articles
- Generic "what is AI" content

## 5. China AI Hub View (author view section)

- Synthesizes evidence; identifies meaningful patterns; explains trade-offs; acknowledges uncertainty; explains practical implications.
- Traceable to the evidence; never promotes or blindly attacks a company; never declares universal winners; never pretends certainty.
- Clearly separated from factual sections (own heading).

## 6. Comparison philosophy

Explain: What differs? Why? For which workloads does it matter? What are the trade-offs? What evidence supports the difference?

- No "A > B" except for a specific measurable metric with source + date.
- No overall rankings. No Best/Winner/#1/Worst.
- Comparison pages explain trade-offs and differences.

## 7. FAQ policy

FAQ sections only when: genuine user questions exist, they add search utility, answers are not duplicates of main content. Never a boilerplate FAQ block on every page.

## 8. Images

No stock AI images. Prefer: charts, data visualizations, architecture diagrams, entity relationship diagrams, original explanatory graphics. Only when they improve understanding.

## 9. Page quality gate (all page types, pre-publication)

- Factual accuracy (source-checked) ✔
- Source quality + freshness ✔
- Originality (editorial) ✔
- Completeness (entity schemas filled or draft status) ✔
- Entity consistency (IDs resolve, versions consistent) ✔
- Internal links present + semantic ✔
- Schema.org JSON-LD valid ✔
- SEO title + meta description + canonical ✔
- Indexability ✔
- Tables correct (headers, captions, responsive) ✔
- Pricing correct (currency/unit/date/source) ✔
- Benchmarks correct (version/score/method/source_type) ✔
- No unsupported claims, no fabricated data ✔

## 10. Length guidance

- Entity pages: whatever the verified data requires — never padded.
- Guides/explainers: 800–2,000 words.
- Comparisons: 1,500–3,000 words, table-heavy.
- Research: 2,000–4,000 words with original analysis; word count never a goal in itself.
